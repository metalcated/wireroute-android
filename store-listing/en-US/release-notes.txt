Initial release of WireRoute for Android.

• Create, import, scan, edit, and export VPN profiles.
• Choose split or full tunnel routing per profile.
• Use profile DNS or encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS.
• Review live traffic and local connection history.
• Explore public endpoints with an optional map lookup.
• Choose Nordic Blue or Android System colors.

Requires your own WireGuard-compatible endpoint, including supported RouterOS configurations.
