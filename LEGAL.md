## Map and endpoint-location services

The Android map uses the open-source MapLibre Native renderer. Its map resources are served by OpenFreeMap and contain OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data; required attribution remains available in the map. After explicit confirmation, the optional endpoint-location preview uses `ipwho.is` to approximate the selected profile's public endpoint location. These independently operated services are subject to their own terms, licenses, and privacy policies.
